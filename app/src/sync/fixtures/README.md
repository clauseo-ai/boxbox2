# fixtures for the sync reducer

- `think-turn.json`: the message, part and delta events of one `[bench:think 40 100]` turn, recorded from a real cmx2 0.8.3 dev daemon at the pin through its `/event` stream, as the daemon sent them (`seq` is the daemon's, which the server's journal carries as `upstream`). a thinking step, a `git status` tool call, a second thinking step and a streamed answer. what it shows: reasoning arrives once, whole, when its step lands; text streams by deltas whose first one (offset 0) is the part's birth; the final parts of the answer message arrive after its text has streamed, reasoning first by id.

only the working directory (`/work`) and the host name (`bench-host`) were substituted. the test that reads it (`reducer.test.ts`) turns each event into the protocol 3 journal entry the server writes for it, its parts and infos made neutral by `neutral.ts`.

- `cache-v3.json`, `cache-v4.json`, `cache-v5.json`: the worker's indexeddb as the builds before v4, v5 and v6 left it (every record of every store, by key) and what that build's `cache.ts` read back from it (`loaded`: the boot and every thread's pages). v4's was captured from main's `cache.ts` at v4, through `Saver`, over the contract 1 replay `saves.ts` held then, with the transcripts cut to their first few messages and two answers of 1500 characters streamed into the first opened thread, the second still streaming, saved every seventh action. v5's was captured the same way from the last v5 build (commit a5298d1) with one answer of 1500 characters left streaming, plus a draft with a photo, a failed send in the outbox, a telemetry row, a reply's record and a prefs record. their fleets and transcripts are contract 1 and cmx2 shapes: `saves.test.ts` opens each with the current build (v6) and expects the fleet, the threads and the cursor gone and the drafts, photos, outbox and prefs kept record for record (docs/protocol.md, the app's indexeddb).
- `neutral.ts`: a neutral thread and fleet for the tests, and cmx2's recorded parts, message infos and messages turned into what the server's `translate.rs` makes of them, so a recorded fixture is ported through it and never rewritten by hand.
- `saves.ts`: the protocol 3 replay the save tests use: `server/goldens/protocol3/journal.sse.gz` behind a hello and a reset, its two busiest threads opened and a third held, each read from a recorded transcript made neutral by `neutral.ts`.
- `two-wire-main.json.gz`: what main (commit cea0b99, contract 1) derives from the recorded tape, for `two-wire.test.ts` (docs/protocol.md, section 16, one recording, two wires). recorded in a checkout of cea0b99: `server/goldens/contract1/tape.jsonl.gz` replayed by `fleet/replay.rs`'s `Replay::new` (a test added there for the recording, `cargo test -p boxbox`, writing each frame of `Replay::journal()` as one json line: 3649 entries); that journal driven through main's `reduce` behind a hello and an empty reset, every thread with transcript entries opened (the first 40) or held (the rest) before its first entry with a read answering nothing, later reads and refetches answered at once from the journal so far; then, from the final state put into the store (`bun test --conditions browser`), home's window keys at the tape's last clock, each window's `windowView`, `windowName`, `tailLines` and `runOf`, and per thread with messages what `createThreadData` builds (turns after `all()`, children, kin, title, directory, repo root, count, working), the ruler and turns panel rows as `stage.tsx` derives them, and what details reads from `details-model.ts`, all with this test's code over main's modules. the final fleet was then made neutral on this branch with `fleet/replay/protocol3.rs`'s `thread_of` and `ask_of` and `translate::agent` and `translate::connection` (another test added for the recording), keyed as the reducer keys them. nothing in it was edited by hand; capabilities `translate::agent` gained after the recording (`btw`) are laid onto its agents by the test (`ADDED_SINCE_RECORDING`).
- `server.ts`: `fakeServer`, a fetch-level fake for the worker's tests; and `JournalServer`, protocol 3 simulated over a journal for the wire replays: per stream the overview and the interest's transcripts (seqs skip), interest posts confirmed at the head behind the entering keys' catch-up (`compactEntries`, the rule of server/src/fleet/catchup.rs, or raw), a dial with a cursor answered by the compact catch-up and `caught`, one without by a reset, reads as the journal built the thread (stamped with the head, the upstream reached and an etag), and settled text journaled as `textOf` (`settleText`).
- `wire-replay.ts`: the protocol 3 wire replay (`wire.test.ts`): the journal tape and the cases it lacks, through `JournalServer`, the reducer, the page gate, the landing and the store on a seeded schedule, checked exactly once against the server's reads.
